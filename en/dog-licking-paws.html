Why does my dog keep licking its paws?

Paw licking may reflect itch, pain or irritation. Allergies are common, but yeast or bacterial infection, a broken nail, foxtail/foreign material and orthopedic pain can look similar.

Veterinarian reviewedUpdated Aug 20, 2026
Quick answer

Check between the toes and around the nails. Redness, swelling, odor, discharge, limping or one paw being much worse than the others deserves veterinary evaluation.

When allergies are more likely

Allergic dogs often affect multiple paws and may also scratch the ears, face, armpits or belly. Recurrent ear infections and seasonal flares can provide useful clues.

When a local problem is more likely

Sudden licking of one foot can suggest a thorn, cut, broken nail, interdigital problem or pain. Carefully inspect without digging into painful tissue.

Why licking can worsen the problem

Repeated moisture and self-trauma can damage the skin barrier and encourage secondary infection, creating an itch–lick cycle.
