VetWel Education Center

Editorial policy and source standards

VetWel educational content is designed to provide clear, clinically responsible, source-aware information for pet owners and veterinary professionals. Product visibility never takes priority over safety, accurate context, or appropriate veterinary care.

Core principle: The value of a health article is measured by whether it helps the reader understand the issue and the appropriate next step—not simply by whether it attracts search traffic. Urgent, diagnostic, and treatment decisions come before product discussion.
01 • Expertise

Veterinary perspective and authorship

Health content identifies veterinary authorship or veterinary contribution whenever appropriate. The veterinary perspective of the VetWel Education Center is supported by Veterinarian Nazif Oben Akşemsettinoğlu.

02 • Sources

Source selection

Priority is given to credible veterinary and academic sources relevant to the topic. Depending on the subject, these may include veterinary colleges, professional veterinary organizations, clinical references, guidelines, and peer-reviewed literature.

  • Veterinary universities and teaching hospitals
  • Professional veterinary organizations and clinical guidance
  • Peer-reviewed research and established veterinary references
03 • Safety

Education does not replace veterinary care

VetWel educational content is general information. It does not replace veterinary examination, diagnosis, prescription treatment, emergency care, or disease-specific management.

Potential emergencies such as urinary obstruction, breathing difficulty, seizures, major trauma, or suspected toxin exposure are prioritized as urgent veterinary situations before any supportive-product discussion.

04 • Products

Product-positioning standards

VetWel products are included only when there is a clinically sensible complementary-support relationship to the topic. Products are not positioned as substitutes for diagnosis or treatment, and absolute outcomes are avoided when evidence does not justify them.

05 • Evidence

Evidence boundaries

Ingredient-level or mechanistic literature is not presented as proof that a finished VetWel formula diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents disease. Product-specific evidence, broader ingredient evidence, and formulation rationale are distinguished whenever relevant.

06 • Updates

Content review and update dates

Articles may be reviewed when clinical guidance, references, safety information, or product information changes. A modification date is updated when a meaningful content change occurs—not simply to make a page appear newer.

07 • AI Assistance

Digital and AI-assisted tools

Digital or AI-assisted tools may support research organization, drafting, editing, or content operations. They do not replace veterinary source review, clinical context, editorial responsibility, or safety checks.

08 • Corrections

Correction policy

If a meaningful error, outdated statement, or safety concern is identified, the content is corrected. Health and safety corrections receive priority. Editorial feedback can be sent to info@vetwel.us.

U.S. content standard: The English-language Education Center is written for U.S. pet-owner search intent and U.S. availability context while preserving VetWel’s veterinary-first safety and evidence boundaries.
