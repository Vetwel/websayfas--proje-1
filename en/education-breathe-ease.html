VETWEL® PRODUCT INFORMATION

15 g SACHET

Breathe Ease®

Breathe Ease® is a complementary nutritional formula designed to support respiratory comfort, bronchial wellness, antioxidant defenses, and normal respiratory function in cats and dogs.

What Is It Used For?

Designed as part of a complementary nutritional approach when additional respiratory-system support is appropriate.

Respiratory & Bronchial Support

The formula combines selected nutritional and botanical ingredients to support bronchial wellness and everyday respiratory comfort.

Antioxidant Support

Chlorella and other functional ingredients complement support for normal cellular antioxidant defenses.

Normal Immune Function

Selected botanical and nutritional components complement normal immune function without positioning the product as an immune “booster.”

When May It Be Considered?

  • When respiratory wellness support is desired
  • To complement normal bronchial function
  • During seasonal or environmental respiratory sensitivity
  • As part of a veterinarian-directed supportive-care plan

Veterinary Approach

Breathing difficulty, persistent coughing, or marked respiratory changes require veterinary assessment. Breathe Ease® does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent respiratory disease.

Ingredients & Formulation Rationale

  • Propolis: a naturally derived component of the botanical support system.
  • Chitosan: a functional ingredient included in the broader nutritional-support approach.
  • Chlorella vulgaris: complements nutritional and antioxidant support.
  • Dandelion (Taraxacum): part of the botanical-support blend.
  • Pear Extract: part of the selected botanical combination.
  • Magnesium Oxide: mineral component of the formula.

General Ingredients

Propolis, Chitosan, Chlorella vulgaris, Dandelion (Taraxacum), Pear Extract, and Magnesium Oxide.

Directions for Use

Breathe Ease® is supplied in 15 g sachets.

Cats: 1 sachet daily.

Dogs: approximately 1 sachet per 5 kg (11 lb) body weight daily.

  • Up to 5 kg: 1 sachet
  • 5–10 kg: 2 sachets
  • 10–15 kg: 3 sachets
  • 15–20 kg: 4 sachets

Daily amount and duration may be adjusted according to the animal's clinical status and veterinary assessment.

Brief Product Summary

Breathe Ease® combines botanical, functional, and nutritional ingredients to support respiratory comfort, bronchial wellness, antioxidant defenses, and normal respiratory function in cats and dogs. It is a complementary nutritional product and does not replace veterinary diagnosis or treatment.
