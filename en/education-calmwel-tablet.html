VETWEL® PRODUCT INFORMATION

TABLET

CalmWel®

CalmWel® Tablet is a multi-botanical complementary formula developed to support calm behavior, stress and fear response, behavioral balance, relaxation, and adaptation to changing environmental conditions in cats and dogs.

What Is It Used For?

CalmWel® Tablet takes a broader behavioral-support approach than simple “calming.” It is designed to support stress and fear response, over-arousal, behavioral balance, and environmental adaptation together.

Stress & Fear Response

Supports a more manageable behavioral response to triggers such as noise, travel, veterinary visits, temporary separation, moving, and unfamiliar environments.

Behavioral Balance

Provides complementary support during periods of restlessness, over-arousal, and stress-related behavioral changes.

Environmental Adaptation

Contains botanical ingredients selected to support adjustment to travel, boarding, grooming, veterinary visits, new surroundings, and routine changes.

Relaxation & Tension Support

The multi-botanical formulation is designed to support relaxation and behavioral comfort when tension accompanies stress-related restlessness.

Non-Sedating Goal

The primary objective is not to produce marked sedation. The goal is to support calmer, more balanced behavior while preserving normal daily activity as much as possible.

When May It Be Considered?

  • During travel and transportation
  • Before and after veterinary visits
  • During boarding or grooming
  • During moving or environmental changes
  • During noise-related fear responses
  • For stress-related restlessness associated with temporary separation
  • During periods of excessive barking or over-arousal
  • As part of a veterinarian-directed plan for stress-related repetitive behaviors
  • When stress contributes to inappropriate elimination or marking
  • For reproductive-cycle-related restlessness when considered appropriate by a veterinarian

Ingredients & Formulation Rationale

CalmWel® Tablet is not based on a single calming herb. Its multi-botanical phytocomplex is designed to support interconnected areas of behavior including calmness, fear and stress response, behavioral balance, environmental adaptation, and tension management.

Calmness Pathway

Valeriana officinalis, Humulus lupulus, and Melissa officinalis form a core botanical group supporting calm behavior, relaxation, and tension response.

Fear & Over-Arousal

The broader phytocomplex provides multi-botanical support intended to help moderate excessive responses to predictable or environmental stressors.

Behavioral Balance

Leonurus, Humulus, Valeriana, and complementary botanicals are included to support a more balanced response during restlessness and over-arousal.

Adaptation Pathway

Melissa officinalis, Nepeta cataria, and Crataegus complement the formulation's environmental-adaptation and relaxation strategy.

Relaxation & Tension

Valeriana, Humulus, Melissa, Leonurus, and other botanicals are used together within the relaxation and behavioral-comfort pathway.

Formulation approach: CalmWel® Tablet is not intended to sedate the animal. Its core strategy is to support calmness → stress and fear response → behavioral balance → environmental adaptation through several complementary botanical pathways.

Veterinary Approach

Behavioral changes should not automatically be attributed to “stress.” Pain, endocrine disease, neurologic conditions, urinary or gastrointestinal disorders, and environmental factors can produce similar changes.

New, recurrent, or severe behavioral changes should therefore be evaluated by a veterinarian to identify and address possible underlying causes.

Behavior Management

CalmWel® Tablet should not be considered a stand-alone solution for phobias, aggression, separation-related behavior problems, or compulsive behaviors.

When indicated, it should be used together with environmental modification, behavior modification, and other veterinarian-recommended approaches.

Use & Safety Note

  • Discontinue use if individual sensitivity to any ingredient is suspected.
  • Consult a veterinarian before use during pregnancy or lactation.
  • Use in young animals should be based on veterinary assessment.
  • Inform the veterinarian if the animal is receiving behavior-modifying products or medications that affect the central nervous system.
  • Because the formula contains Hypericum perforatum, veterinary review is advisable in animals receiving ongoing medications due to potential interactions.

Directions for Use

CalmWel® Tablet is administered orally.

Cats: 1 tablet per administration.

Dogs:

  • 1–10 kg: 1 tablet
  • 10–20 kg: 2 tablets
  • 20–30 kg: 3 tablets
  • 30–40 kg: 4 tablets
  • 40–50 kg: 5 tablets
  • 50–120 kg: 5 tablets
First 3 days

Administer 3 times daily: morning – midday – evening.

From day 4 onward

Administer 2 times daily: morning – evening.

Minimum duration

At least 21 days.

Duration and protocol may be individualized according to behavioral response and veterinary assessment.

Brief Product Summary

CalmWel® Tablet is a multi-botanical behavioral-support formula designed to support stress and fear response without relying on sedation as its primary approach. It combines pathways for calmness, behavioral balance, environmental adaptation, and tension management, with the goal of supporting a more manageable behavioral response while preserving normal daily activity as much as possible.
