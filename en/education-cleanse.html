VETWEL® PROFESSIONAL PRODUCT INFORMATION

VETERINARY-USE LIQUID

Cleanse®

Cleanse® is a botanical liquid intended for complementary urinary-system support within veterinarian-directed clinical care. Professional-use information on this page is not intended for pet-owner self-treatment.

Professional Use Context

Cleanse® is intended to complement veterinary management when urinary-flow, sediment-management, natural-elimination, and urinary-comfort support are considered appropriate.

Urinary Flow Support

Selected botanicals are included as part of a formulation strategy supporting normal urinary flow and natural elimination.

Sediment-Management Support

The formula may be considered within a veterinarian-directed urinary-care plan when urinary sediment or crystal-associated findings are present.

Post-Procedure Support

Where clinically appropriate, a veterinarian may incorporate the product into post-procedure supportive care following standard urinary management.

Ingredients & Formulation Rationale

The liquid formulation combines selected botanicals around the support pathway urinary flow → sediment management → natural elimination → urinary comfort.

Polygonum aviculare

Included as part of the urinary-flow and natural-elimination support strategy.

Ononis arvensis

Included in the broader botanical approach to urinary-system support.

Equisetum arvense

Included to complement normal diuresis and urinary elimination.

Polygonum persicaria

Complements the multi-botanical urinary-support formulation.

Important: Cleanse® does not replace emergency stabilization, analgesia, catheterization, correction of electrolyte abnormalities, imaging, or any other required veterinary treatment for acute urethral obstruction.

Veterinary Approach

Urethral obstruction is a medical emergency. Hydration, electrolyte status, renal function, cardiovascular status, and bladder integrity must be assessed and managed according to standard veterinary practice.

Cleanse® should only be considered after stabilization and clinical assessment by a veterinarian.

For Veterinary Professionals Only

Any catheter-associated, intravesical, or post-procedure use must be performed or directed by a veterinarian. The Turkish professional protocol currently includes catheter-directed local use, optional bladder administration after patency is established, and short-term post-procedure oral support. Exact use should be individualized to the patient and the veterinarian's clinical judgment.

Never for owner-performed catheterization

Pet owners should not attempt urethral catheterization, bladder instillation, or other invasive administration.

Critical Safety Note

  • Acute obstruction requires immediate veterinary intervention.
  • Hyperkalemia, postrenal azotemia, dehydration, pain, and other complications require independent management.
  • Recurrent cases require investigation of underlying causes.
  • Appropriate diet and water intake remain part of long-term urinary management.

Brief Product Summary

Cleanse® is a veterinary-use botanical liquid for complementary urinary-system support. It may be incorporated by veterinarians into selected urinary-care protocols, but it is not a treatment substitute for acute urethral obstruction and must never delay emergency stabilization, catheterization, or other required medical care.
