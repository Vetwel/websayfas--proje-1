VETWEL® PRODUCT INFORMATION

LIQUID

KidneyWel®

KidneyWel® Liquid is a multi-botanical complementary liquid formula developed to support renal function, diuresis, natural elimination of nitrogenous metabolites, urinary flow, and urinary comfort in cats and dogs.

What Is It Used For?

KidneyWel® Liquid is designed to complement veterinary care by supporting normal renal function, urinary flow, natural elimination processes, and renal metabolic balance.

Renal Function

Provides multi-botanical nutritional support intended to complement normal renal physiology and renal wellness.

Diuresis & Natural Elimination

One formulation pathway is designed to support normal urine production and the body's natural urinary elimination of nitrogenous metabolites.

Urinary System Support

Contains botanical ingredients selected to complement urinary flow, urinary comfort, and mineral/debris management.

Metabolic & Antioxidant Support

The multi-ingredient approach is designed to complement metabolic balance and normal cellular antioxidant defenses as part of a broader renal wellness plan.

When May It Be Considered?

  • When longer-term renal support is recommended by a veterinarian
  • When diuresis and natural elimination support are desired
  • When urinary flow and urinary-system support are appropriate
  • As complementary support within a veterinarian-directed renal care plan
  • When support for the natural elimination of nitrogenous metabolites is desired
  • Alongside veterinary-directed renal nutrition and treatment plans

Ingredients & Formulation Rationale

KidneyWel® Liquid is not designed around a single renal mechanism. Its multi-botanical formulation brings together complementary support pathways for renal function, diuresis, natural elimination, urinary flow, urinary comfort, mineral/debris management, and metabolic balance.

Equisetum arvense

Included as a core botanical in the formulation's diuresis and urinary-support strategy.

Lespedeza capitata

Included in the phytotherapeutic approach to renal support and management of nitrogenous metabolic load.

Orthosiphon stamineus

Complements the formulation's urinary-flow and natural-elimination support pathway.

Rubia tinctorum

Included as part of the approach to urinary mineral/debris management and normal passage.

Phaseolus vulgaris

Provides secondary support for reducing gastrointestinal phosphorus absorption as one component of the broader formulation.

Berberis vulgaris

Included as part of the botanical approach to urinary wellness and normal inflammatory balance.

Urtica dioica

Complements urinary elimination and botanical antioxidant support.

Taraxacum officinale

Included as complementary botanical support for fluid balance, natural elimination, and general metabolic wellness.

The core formulation strategy is to support the renal and urinary systems through multiple complementary pathways rather than a single target.

Important: Phosphorus is not the primary formulation target of KidneyWel® Liquid.

The primary support pathway is: renal function → diuresis → natural elimination of nitrogenous metabolites → urinary flow and comfort.

Support for reducing gastrointestinal phosphorus absorption is a secondary component of the broader renal strategy. KidneyWel® is not a phosphate binder.

Veterinary Approach

Complementary renal support should be evaluated together with the patient's renal function, hydration status, nutrition, urine output, concurrent conditions, and current therapies.

KidneyWel® Liquid does not replace veterinary diagnosis, renal diets, fluid therapy, prescription medications, or other veterinarian-directed treatment. It should be considered only as complementary support within an individualized renal care plan.

Veterinary Monitoring Considerations

  • Hydration status
  • Urine output and urinary signs
  • BUN trend
  • Creatinine and/or SDMA
  • Serum phosphorus
  • Appetite and body weight
  • Urinalysis when indicated
  • Adherence to the prescribed renal diet
  • Overall clinical response

Directions for Use

KidneyWel® Liquid is supplied in liquid form.

Cats and dogs: 0.5 mL/kg body weight per administration.

Quick Dose Examples
  • 2 kg: 1 mL
  • 4 kg: 2 mL
  • 5 kg: 2.5 mL
  • 10 kg: 5 mL
  • 20 kg: 10 mL
Frequency

Administer twice daily.

Administration

May be administered directly by mouth or mixed with food or drinking water.

Duration of use: determined by the veterinarian based on clinical evaluation.

Brief Product Summary

KidneyWel® Liquid is a multi-botanical liquid formula developed to provide broad renal and urinary support. Its primary formulation pathway focuses on supporting renal function, diuresis, natural elimination of nitrogenous metabolites, urinary flow, and urinary comfort. Support for reducing gastrointestinal phosphorus absorption is secondary; KidneyWel® is not a phosphate binder. It does not replace a renal diet or a veterinarian-directed clinical care plan.
