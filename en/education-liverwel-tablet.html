VETWEL® PRODUCT INFORMATION

TABLET

LiverWel®

LiverWel® Tablet is a multi-botanical complementary formula containing 21 botanical extracts, developed to support normal hepatic function together with biliary physiology, antioxidant defenses, digestive function, metabolic balance, and natural elimination processes in cats and dogs.

What Is It Used For?

LiverWel® Tablet is formulated to complement veterinary care by supporting the liver's normal metabolic and functional capacity while also addressing biliary, digestive, antioxidant, and natural elimination support.

Hepatic Function

The liver is central to many metabolic processes. LiverWel® provides complementary botanical support for normal hepatic metabolism and liver function.

Biliary & Digestive Support

The hepatobiliary component of the formula includes selected botanicals intended to support normal bile-system physiology and digestive function.

Antioxidant Defense

Because the liver is metabolically active and exposed to oxidative load, selected botanical ingredients are included to support normal cellular antioxidant defenses.

Elimination & Metabolic Balance

LiverWel® is not positioned around a vague “detox” concept. Its broader approach supports natural elimination, fluid balance, digestion, and normal metabolic processes together.

When May It Be Considered?

  • When additional hepatic support is appropriate
  • As complementary support within a veterinarian-directed liver care plan
  • During periods of increased metabolic demand
  • When biliary and digestive support are desired
  • For senior or chronically managed cats and dogs requiring hepatic wellness support
  • When appetite and digestive comfort are important parts of supportive care

Ingredients & Formulation Rationale

LiverWel® Tablet is not simply a “liver vitamin.” Its 21-extract phytocomplex is designed around multiple complementary pathways: hepatic function, biliary and digestive support, antioxidant defenses, natural elimination, metabolic balance, and digestive comfort.

Hepatic Function Pathway

Taraxacum officinale, Helichrysum arenarium, and Glycyrrhiza glabra form part of the botanical group selected to support normal hepatic metabolism and liver function.

Biliary & Digestive Pathway

Helichrysum arenarium, Taraxacum officinale, Achillea millefolium, and Foeniculum vulgare are included to complement biliary and gastrointestinal function.

Antioxidant Defense Pathway

Urtica dioica, Echinacea purpurea, Viola tricolor, and Glechoma hederacea contribute to the formulation's botanical antioxidant-support strategy.

Elimination & Metabolic Balance

Betula pendula, Galium aparine, and Urtica dioica complement natural elimination, fluid balance, and metabolic support.

Appetite & Digestive Comfort

Centaurium erythraea, Chamomilla recutita, Foeniculum vulgare, and Mentha pulegium form a complementary pathway supporting appetite and digestive comfort.

Formulation approach: LiverWel® Tablet is not positioned solely as a “detox” product. Its core strategy is to support hepatic function → biliary and digestive function → antioxidant defenses → natural elimination and metabolic balance as interconnected areas of liver wellness.

Veterinary Approach

Elevated liver enzymes alone are not a diagnosis. When hepatobiliary disease is suspected, clinical signs, biochemical parameters, imaging findings, nutrition, medications, and concurrent conditions should be evaluated together.

LiverWel® Tablet does not replace diagnosis, an appropriate hepatic diet, or veterinarian-directed hepatobiliary treatment. It should be considered only as complementary hepatic support within an individualized veterinary care plan.

Veterinary Monitoring Considerations

Depending on the patient's clinical status, a veterinarian may consider monitoring:

  • ALT and AST trends
  • ALP and GGT
  • Total bilirubin
  • Albumin and total protein
  • Appetite and body weight
  • Vomiting, stool quality, and digestive status
  • Hydration and overall clinical status
  • Bile acids and further hepatobiliary evaluation when indicated
  • Clinical and laboratory response during longer-term use

Formula & Safety Note

Because the formula contains Hypericum perforatum and Glycyrrhiza glabra, use in patients receiving concurrent medications should be evaluated by a veterinarian.

In patients with suspected biliary-flow disorders or serious systemic disease, the support plan should be individualized.

For longer-term use, veterinary follow-up and appropriate laboratory monitoring are recommended.

Directions for Use

LiverWel® Tablet is supplied in tablet form.

Cats: 1 tablet per administration.

Dogs:

  • 1–10 kg: 1 tablet
  • 10–20 kg: 2 tablets
  • 20–30 kg: 3 tablets
  • 30–40 kg: 4 tablets
  • 40–50 kg: 5 tablets
  • 50–120 kg: 5 tablets
First 3 days

Administer 3 times daily: morning – midday – evening.

From day 4 onward

Administer 2 times daily: morning – evening.

Minimum duration of use: at least 21 days.

Duration may be extended based on the patient's clinical status and the veterinarian's assessment.

Brief Product Summary

LiverWel® Tablet is a multi-botanical hepatic support formula containing 21 botanical extracts. It is designed to support hepatic function through several interconnected pathways, including biliary and digestive function, antioxidant defenses, natural elimination, and metabolic balance. It is intended as complementary support and does not replace primary hepatobiliary treatment or a veterinarian-directed care plan.
