VETWEL® PRODUCT INFORMATION

SPECIALIZED NUTRITIONAL SUPPORT

Malign Detox®

Malign Detox® combines functional mushrooms and a broad botanical complex to provide complementary nutritional, antioxidant, metabolic, and general-condition support for cats and dogs requiring additional veterinary-managed care.

Supportive-Care Role

Designed to complement a veterinarian-directed care plan by supporting nutritional status, antioxidant defenses, general condition, and quality of life.

Functional Mushroom Complex

Shiitake, Reishi, and Chaga form the core functional-mushroom group within the formulation.

Botanical Support

A broad botanical complex complements normal metabolic function, digestive support, antioxidant defenses, and natural elimination.

Antioxidant Support

Mushroom and botanical ingredients contribute to support for normal cellular antioxidant defenses during periods of increased physiologic demand.

General Condition

The goal is not to target a tumor directly, but to complement veterinary care with broader nutritional and quality-of-life support.

When May It Be Considered?

  • As complementary nutritional support during oncology care
  • In selected chronic or special-care patients
  • When general condition and quality-of-life support are priorities
  • When antioxidant support is considered appropriate
  • Within an individualized veterinarian-directed supportive-care program

Ingredients & Formulation Rationale

Malign Detox® combines several functional mushrooms with a broad botanical phytocomplex rather than relying on a single ingredient.

Functional Mushroom Pathway

Shiitake, Reishi, and Chaga form the core mushroom complex for broad physiologic and nutritional support.

Botanical Antioxidant Pathway

Echinacea, calendula, nettle, burdock, and other botanicals complement normal cellular antioxidant defenses.

Metabolic & Elimination Support

Birch, horsetail, Galium, and selected botanicals support normal fluid balance and natural elimination processes.

Gastrointestinal Support

Licorice, lemon balm, meadowsweet, yarrow, and complementary botanicals support digestive comfort and nutritional status.

Broccoli Seed

Broccoli seed complements the formulation's plant-based functional-nutrition approach.

Important: Malign Detox® is not a cancer treatment and is not intended to treat tumors or metastasis. Its support pathway is general condition → nutritional support → antioxidant defenses → metabolic balance → quality of life, alongside the veterinarian's primary treatment plan.

Veterinary Approach

In oncology patients, supportive products should be evaluated in the context of tumor type, disease stage, organ function, medications, surgery plans, and overall clinical status. Malign Detox® does not replace surgery, chemotherapy, radiation therapy, targeted therapy, immunotherapy, or other veterinarian-recommended oncology treatment.

Important Safety & Interaction Notes

  • Hypericum perforatum: may have clinically relevant interaction potential with many medications.
  • Use during chemotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy should be planned with the treating veterinarian.
  • Anticoagulants, NSAIDs, opioids, anticonvulsants, and sedatives warrant interaction review.
  • Perioperative use should be reviewed before surgery.
  • Chaga: requires particular caution in patients with kidney disease.
  • Symphytum: should be evaluated carefully in patients with liver disease.
  • Trifolium pratense: requires careful veterinary review in hormone-sensitive tumors.

Directions for Use

No fixed universal dose or duration is presented for Malign Detox®.

Dose & frequency

Must be individualized by the veterinarian according to tumor type, current treatment, clinical status, liver and kidney function, and concurrent medications.

Duration

Should be determined according to clinical response and the primary treatment protocol.

All medications and supplements should be disclosed to the treating veterinarian before use in an actively treated oncology patient.

Brief Product Summary

Malign Detox® combines Shiitake, Reishi, Chaga, broccoli seed, and a broad botanical phytocomplex for specialized nutritional support. Its purpose is not to treat cancer, tumors, or metastasis, but to complement veterinarian-directed care by supporting general condition, nutrition, antioxidant defenses, metabolic balance, and quality of life. Because of potential drug interactions, use should be individualized by the treating veterinarian.
