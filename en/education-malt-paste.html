VETWEL® PRODUCT INFORMATION

MALT PASTE

VetWel® Malt Paste

VetWel® Malt Paste combines oils, fiber, and prebiotic ingredients to support the natural gastrointestinal passage of swallowed hair, normal intestinal motility, and gastrointestinal wellness, especially in cats.

What Is It Used For?

Developed primarily to help swallowed hair move through the gastrointestinal tract and be eliminated naturally in cats.

Hairball Support

The oil-and-fiber combination supports movement of swallowed hair through the intestinal contents and natural elimination in the stool.

Normal Intestinal Motility

Plant fibers support normal stool bulk and gastrointestinal motility.

Prebiotic Support

Corn dextrin provides a prebiotic substrate as part of the formula's intestinal-microbiota support approach.

Palatability

Malt extract, protein hydrolysate, and catnip help support product acceptance and convenient daily use.

When May It Be Considered?

  • Cats prone to hairballs
  • During periods of heavy shedding
  • Routine care for long-haired cats
  • When normal intestinal motility requires support
  • For prebiotic gastrointestinal support
  • In selected dogs only when specifically considered appropriate by a veterinarian

Ingredients & Formulation Rationale

VetWel® Malt Paste is more than a lubricating malt product. It combines oil, fiber, and prebiotic support to address hair passage and normal intestinal function through complementary mechanisms.

Oil & Coating Pathway

Vegetable food oil, castor oil, and petrolatum oil provide lubricating and softening support to help facilitate natural passage of swallowed hair.

Fiber Pathway

Plant food fibers support normal stool bulk and gastrointestinal motility.

Prebiotic Pathway

Corn dextrin provides a prebiotic substrate intended to support intestinal microbiota.

Acceptance & Palatability

Malt extract, protein hydrolysate, and catnip support convenient oral acceptance.

Formulation approach: softening/coating of swallowed hair → fiber support → normal intestinal motility → natural elimination, with prebiotic ingredients complementing gastrointestinal wellness.

Veterinary Approach

Recurrent vomiting, poor appetite, abdominal pain, inability to defecate, or deterioration in general condition should not automatically be attributed to hairballs. Foreign-body ingestion, constipation, obstruction, and other gastrointestinal conditions require veterinary assessment.

VetWel® Malt Paste is not a treatment for mechanical gastrointestinal obstruction.

Important Safety Note

Veterinary assessment is required before use when there is suspicion of a sharp foreign body, string or other linear foreign body, battery ingestion, magnet ingestion, gastrointestinal perforation, severe or recurrent vomiting, marked abdominal pain, inability to defecate, or gastrointestinal obstruction. Fresh drinking water should always be available.

Directions for Use

Cats over 2 kg: 5 cm once daily.

Cats 2 kg or less: 2 cm once daily.

Kittens: 2 cm daily.

Ferrets: 2 cm daily.

Heavy shedding

The daily amount may be doubled when appropriate.

Typical use period

An approximately 1-month program may be used and repeated during spring/fall shedding periods as appropriate.

Dogs

No routine fixed hairball dose is recommended for dogs. Consider use only in selected cases under veterinary guidance.

Storage

Refrigerate the opened tube.

Brief Product Summary

VetWel® Malt Paste combines oils, fiber, and prebiotic ingredients to support the natural gastrointestinal passage of swallowed hair, normal intestinal motility, and intestinal microbiota, especially in cats. It should not be used as a substitute for veterinary assessment when gastrointestinal obstruction or dangerous foreign-body ingestion is suspected.
