SkinWel®

SkinWel® is a multi-botanical dermatologic support formula containing 20 botanical ingredients, developed to support skin-barrier integrity, skin comfort, healthy epithelial renewal, local defenses, and coat quality in cats and dogs.

What Is It Used For?

SkinWel® is formulated to complement veterinary dermatologic care by supporting skin health and barrier integrity, helping maintain skin comfort during periods of sensitivity, redness, or irritation, and supporting healthy coat quality.

Skin Barrier Support

A healthy skin barrier helps limit moisture loss and supports the skin's natural defense against environmental challenges. SkinWel® includes selected botanicals intended to complement normal barrier integrity.

Skin Comfort

The multi-botanical phytocomplex is designed to support skin comfort and a normal inflammatory response during periods of itching, redness, or sensitivity.

Epithelial Integrity & Renewal

Selected botanicals are included to support normal epithelial renewal, surface integrity, and the skin's natural repair processes.

Coat Health

Healthy skin-barrier function is closely linked with coat quality. SkinWel® also provides complementary support for a healthy, strong, and vibrant coat appearance.

When May It Be Considered?

  • When additional skin-barrier support is appropriate
  • During periods of skin sensitivity, redness, or irritation
  • For dry or sensitive skin
  • When coat quality requires additional nutritional support
  • As complementary support in recurrent dermatologic management plans
  • For longer-term skin and coat wellness support under veterinary guidance

Ingredients & Formulation Rationale

SkinWel® is not designed around a single dermatologic mechanism. Its 20-botanical phytocomplex combines complementary pathways for skin-barrier integrity, skin comfort, normal inflammatory balance, epithelial renewal, local defenses, and coat health.

Inflammatory Balance & Skin Comfort

Calendula officinalis, Chamomilla recutita, Glycyrrhiza glabra, and Filipendula ulmaria form part of the botanical pathway supporting normal inflammatory balance and comfort in sensitive or irritated skin.

Epithelial Renewal & Integrity

Calendula officinalis, Plantago afra, and Achillea millefolium are included to support normal epithelial renewal and skin-surface integrity.

Skin Barrier & Coat Pathway

Arctium majus, Equisetum arvense, and Betula pendula complement the formulation's support for skin-barrier integrity, connective-tissue wellness, and healthy coat quality.

Local Defense & Antioxidant Support

Echinacea purpurea, Urtica dioica, Glechoma hederacea, and Bidens pilosa contribute to the formula's local-defense and botanical antioxidant-support strategy.

The formula also contains Hypericum perforatum, Valeriana officinalis, Quercus robur, Sanguisorba officinalis, and other selected botanicals, providing a broad multi-pathway approach to dermatologic wellness.

Formulation approach: SkinWel® is more than a simple coat supplement. Its core strategy is to support skin-barrier integrity → skin comfort → epithelial renewal → local defenses → healthy coat quality as interconnected areas of dermatologic wellness.

Veterinary Approach

Itching, redness, hair loss, or recurrent dermatologic problems may have many underlying causes. Ectoparasites, bacterial or fungal infections, allergic disease, nutritional factors, endocrine disorders, and environmental triggers should be considered in the veterinary differential diagnosis.

SkinWel® does not replace primary dermatologic diagnosis or treatment. It should be considered as complementary support within a veterinarian-directed dermatologic management plan.

Veterinary Monitoring Considerations

  • Frequency and intensity of itching
  • Degree of redness and irritation
  • Overall skin-barrier appearance
  • Dryness and scaling
  • Hair loss and coat quality
  • Distribution and extent of lesions
  • Signs of secondary infection
  • Overall patient comfort

Directions for Use

SkinWel® is supplied in tablet form.

Cats: 1 tablet per administration.

Dogs: 1 tablet per 10 kg body weight per administration.

  • 1–10 kg: 1 tablet
  • 10–20 kg: 2 tablets
  • 20–30 kg: 3 tablets
  • 30–40 kg: 4 tablets
  • 40–50 kg: 5 tablets
First 3 days

Administer 3 times daily: morning – midday – evening.

From day 4 onward

Administer 2 times daily: morning – evening.

Minimum duration of use: at least 21 days.

Duration may be extended according to the veterinarian's assessment of the patient's dermatologic condition and clinical response.

Brief Product Summary

SkinWel® is a 20-botanical dermatologic support formula designed to support skin-barrier integrity, skin comfort, normal inflammatory balance, epithelial renewal, local defenses, and healthy coat quality together. Rather than focusing on a single dermatologic mechanism, it provides multi-pathway complementary support within a veterinarian-directed skin-care plan.
