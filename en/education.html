Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information about the Education Center.

Where are the U.S. health articles?

The U.S. Health Articles hub contains ten veterinarian-reviewed articles built around priority American pet-owner search topics such as dental health, itching, diarrhea, gut health, anxiety, urinary problems, kidney disease, weight, nutrition, and arthritis.

Where is the Botanical Guide?

The Botanical Guide is available from the main navigation, the Education Center, and individual product pages.

Where can I find scientific references?

Scientific Evidence sections are included within the individual product guides and clearly distinguish product-specific evidence from broader ingredient or mechanistic literature.

Does this information replace veterinary care?

No. Educational content does not replace veterinary examination, diagnosis, or treatment.

Where can U.S. customers purchase VetWel products?

Use the U.S. Where to Buy page for verified online or retail channels as they become available. The English site does not display the Turkish veterinary-clinic directory.
